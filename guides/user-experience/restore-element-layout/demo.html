Feature: display: revert

Restore an element's native layout behavior without manually tracking if it was a block, inline, list-item, or table-cell.

Note: Your browser may not support the revert value. The demo requires a modern browser (Chrome 84+, Firefox 67+, Safari 9.1+).

  • I am an LI: I should restore my list-item behavior and bullet point.

Notice that all elements above are toggled with the exact same class. Using display: revert ensures a <span> doesn't accidentally become a block and a <li> doesn't lose its marker.
